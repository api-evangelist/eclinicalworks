WESTBOROUGH, Mass. — June 4, 2026 — eClinicalWorks®, one of the largest ambulatory cloud EHRs, announced that Neurology Center of New England the largest neurological care center in Massachusetts, is successfully utilizing Sunoh.ai the AI-powered medical scribe, to achieve daily two to four hours of time-savings on documentation and enhance patient engagement. Integrated within the eClinicalWorks EHR, Sunoh.ai enables providers to complete notes during patient encounters, significantly improving work-life balance.
