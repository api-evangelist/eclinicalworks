WESTBOROUGH, Mass. — July 09, 2026 — eClinicalWorks®, one of the largest ambulatory cloud EHR providers, announced that Joi Life Wellness a neurology practice in Georgia specializing in cognitive disorders, is leveraging the AI Assistant for PRISMA to access and review patient records within approximately five minutes from multiple days. The AI-driven health information search engine supports faster clinical decision-making and accelerated care delivery.
