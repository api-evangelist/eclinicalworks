WESTBOROUGH, Mass. — August 13, 2026 — eClinicalWorks®, one of the largest ambulatory cloud EHRs, announced that Bucksport Regional Health Center a Maine-based Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC), is successfully leveraging eClinicalWorks and healow® to improve patient engagement and care access. The FQHC is utilizing the eClinicalWorks patient portal, healow App, and healow TeleVisits™ and has since reported enhancing its virtual patient care by web-enabling 60% of the total patient population.
