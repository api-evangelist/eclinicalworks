WESTBOROUGH, Mass. — September 3, 2026 — eClinicalWorks®, one of the leading ambulatory cloud EHRs, today announces that Pinnacle Orthopaedics & Sports Medicine Specialists a renowned orthopedic practice based in Marietta, GA, has been using healow GenieTM, an AI-powered call center solution to improve operational efficiency, enhance patient engagement, and streamline care for its 80,000 annual patients. The practice manages up to 15,000 calls each month.
