WESTBOROUGH, Mass. — October 1, 2026 — eClinicalWorks®, one of the largest ambulatory cloud EHRs, announced that DFW Pulmonary and Sleep Clinic a Texas-based pulmonary specialty practice, is utilizing healow® patient engagement solutions and Sunoh.ai, the AI medical scribe that seamlessly integrated with the eClinicalWorks EHR. The practice reports Sunoh.ai helps providers save two to three hours daily, complete clinical notes same day, and reduce after-hours charting which further helps accelerate orders and billing workflows.
